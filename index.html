ABOUT

Tony Dang

Tony is an analog photographer based in Prague. His work moves between fashion, portraiture and documentary photography, with an interest in atmosphere, human presence and the imperfections of analog film. Working exclusively with analog photography, he approaches each image with an appreciation for its physical process, unpredictability and slower pace.
